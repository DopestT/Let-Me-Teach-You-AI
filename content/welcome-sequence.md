# Welcome Sequence — 5 Emails

These five emails go into a **Beehiiv Automation** triggered on **new subscription**.
Add each one as an email step and set the send delays to match the cadence below
(immediately, then Day 1, Day 2, Day 4, Day 7 after the previous send / signup).

Before you turn the automation on, replace the placeholders:

- `{{first_name}}` — use Beehiiv's real first-name merge tag (Beehiiv writes this
  as `{{ subscriber.first_name }}` in most cases; the `default:` fallback keeps
  the greeting warm when no name was collected).
- `[DOWNLOAD_LINK]` — the public URL to the PDF
  (`/lead-magnet/Start_Using_AI_Today_25_Beginner_Prompts.pdf`, or a Beehiiv-hosted
  file link).
- `— [Your name]` — sign with the founder's real name.

Tone: warm, plain-spoken, practical, honest. No hype. No jargon. No get-rich-quick
promises.

## Email 2 — Send: Day 1

**Subject:** Why most AI prompts give weak answers
**Preview:** The fix takes about ten seconds.

Hey {{first_name | default: "there"}},

Quick lesson today, because it changes everything.

Most people get weak answers from AI for one simple reason: **they're too vague,
and they stop at the first reply.**

AI can't read your mind. The more specific you are — and the more you ask it to
try again — the better it gets.

Here's a plain example. Instead of:

> "Write a message to my boss."

Try:

> "Write a short, friendly email to my boss asking to move our Thursday meeting to
> Friday morning. Keep it under four sentences and sound warm but professional."

Then, when it replies, don't just accept it. Push once more:

> "Good start. Make it a little more casual and add a thank-you at the end."

Be specific. Add context. Ask it to try again. That three-step habit is 80% of
getting great results.

Give it a shot today with any prompt from your set.

— [Your name]

## Email 3 — Send: Day 2

**Subject:** Use AI to clear one task off your plate today
**Preview:** A 15-minute exercise you can do right now.

Hey {{first_name | default: "there"}},

Reading about AI is fine. Using it is where it clicks. So let's actually clear
something off your plate today — no coding, about 15 minutes.

**Step 1.** Pick one small task you've been putting off. A tricky email. A rough
draft. A list you need to organize. A message you don't know how to word.

**Step 2.** Open any AI chat and describe it plainly, like you're talking to a
helpful coworker:

> "Help me with [task]. Here's the situation: [context]. Here's what I want at the
> end: [result]. Ask me anything you need before you start."

**Step 3.** Read what it gives you, then ask it to adjust until it's right.

That's it. One real task, done with help.

When you finish, hit reply and tell me what you knocked out. I love hearing these —
they tell me what to teach next.

— [Your name]
